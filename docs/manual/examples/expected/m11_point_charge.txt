Assumptions and limits carried by this estimate:
  - Assumption: the multipole expansion (Laplace expansion of 1/|r - R_i|) is used for r < R_i, i.e. the whole 4f density is taken to lie inside the ligand sphere. The nearest ion of this estimate is at 2.400 Angstrom (no radial moments given, so the margin cannot be checked here); the series terminates exactly at k = 6 for an f shell, so the r < R assumption is the only approximation in the angular part.
  - Convention: the 4f basis is the complex spherical harmonic Y_3m with the Condon-Shortley phase, ordered m = -3..3 (matrix index m + 3); the ligand multipole expansion uses the real tesseral harmonics Z_kq (cosine for q > 0, sine for q < 0). The lattice sums L_kq are in e Angstrom^-(k+1) (one factor of e is the ligand charge, the other is the electron charge carried by the prefactor e^2/(4 pi eps0) = 116141 cm^-1 Angstrom).
